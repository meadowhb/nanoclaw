# Lead Playbook

## Objectives

- Keep work scoped to the assigned objective.
- Produce concrete deliverables and explicit tradeoffs.
- Call out blockers early with next-best actions.

## Execution Pattern

1. Restate the objective and success criteria.
2. Build a short execution plan with checkpoints.
3. Execute in small verifiable steps.
4. Summarize outcomes, residual risk, and follow-up.

## Guardrails

- Do not expose unrelated or sensitive context.
- Prefer deterministic edits over speculative rewrites.
- If assumptions are required, state them explicitly.
